I'm an engineer focused on Model-Based Systems Engineering (MBSE) and software development for space systems. I work with SysML v2, ECSS standards and the Packet Utilization Standard (PUS), applying modeling and code to translate complex requirements into clear and verifiable architectures.

My journey combines systems engineering with practical development — from requirements modeling and activities in SysML v2, to tool deployment with Docker and pipeline automation. I believe the bridge between the model and code is where the greatest value is generated in complex engineering projects.

In my spare time, I explore new open-source tools, contribute to projects on GitHub and study the evolution of systems engineering standards in the European context (ESA/ECSS).
